# Zelekha's own documents on the March 2003 economic plan

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `zelekha_plan_2003-03-01.doc` | The original Word file, "עיקרי תכנית כלכלית להוצאת המשק מהמיתון", signed ד"ר רו"ח ירון זליכה. |
| `zelekha_plan_2003-03-01.pdf` | The same text as a PDF, for reading in a browser. |
| `zelekha_lecture_2002-11.pdf` | Lecture "מקורות אי הוודאות במשק והשפעתם על הצמיחה", Kiryat Ono Academic College, November 2002, as a PDF. |

## Where they come from
Zelekha sent both Word files by email on 18.10.2026. He sent them as sources for this page.
The PDFs were made from the Word files (macOS textutil, then Chrome); the text is unchanged.

employer in 2002. Anyone can read these properties from the original `.doc` (File > Properties in Word, or `olefile`).
They can be edited, so they are consistent with the dates; they are not proof of them.

## How this fits the other sources
- In his book "הגווארדיה השחורה" (2008, ch. 1) Zelekha writes that on a Saturday in March 2003 Netanyahu asked him
  to prepare "נייר עקרונות" over the weekend, and that "במהלך סוף השבוע העליתי על הכתב את עיקרי המדיניות החדשה".
- Globes, 6.5.2008, "למי מגיע הקרדיט על 'התוכנית להצלת המשק'?", names Budget Commissioner Uri Yogev and Accountant
  General Nir Gil'ad as the plan's main planners, pushing for it since 2001:
  https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1000339486
- Neither document mentions housing grants or housing policy.
